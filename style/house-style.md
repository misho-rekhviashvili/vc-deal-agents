# House style

Applies to every deliverable: findings, memo, one-pager, IC questions.

## Tone
- Direct, professional, analytical. This is an internal IC document, not a sales pitch.
- State the fact, then what it means for the investment. No adjectives doing the work numbers should do.
- Name risks plainly. A memo that hides a risk is worse than one that rejects the deal.

## Numbers
- Put the evidence in brackets right after the claim, in compact form:
  "the business is scaling quickly (ARR +120% FY24A→FY25E)"
  not "ARR increased from €5.0M in FY24 to €11.0M in FY25".
- Always mark the period type: A (actual), E (estimate / current year), B (budget), F (forecast), M (management case).
- Currency symbol before the number, unit after: €3.2M, $18M, €65M pre money.
- Negative values in parentheses in tables: (€5.4M).
- Multiples with x: 5.0x EV/Revenue, 1.9x MOIC.
- Ranges with an en dash and no spaces: €500–2,000, 2.7–3%. This is the one dash allowed.
- Distinguish management figures from our figures: "management plan" vs "central case".

## Punctuation
- Do not use hyphens (-) or em dashes (—). Use a colon, comma, semicolon, brackets or a new sentence.
  "Mid market, cloud based software" not "mid-market cloud-based software"; "Series A: led by a US fund" not "Series A — led by a US fund".
- Bullets in Markdown are fine (they are structure, not prose).

## Banned
exciting, unique opportunity, game changer, revolutionary, world class, best in class, incredible,
massive, huge, amazing, unparalleled, disruptive, cutting edge, no brainer, once in a lifetime.

## Reference tags (working drafts only)
- Every sentence with a figure or a factual claim about the company carries a tag:
  [F-07] for key findings, [E-03] for external checks, [R-02] for returns outputs.
- Tags are stripped automatically when the deliverables are published to final/.
