# Fund mandate

Read by the memo-writer (Portfolio Fit section) and the ic-partner (mandate check).
This describes an example growth stage fintech fund. Edit it to match your own fund; every deal run reuses this file.

FUND SIZE: €250M committed capital, 10 year term (+2 years extension)
STRATEGY: minority growth equity in technology companies, through direct investments, co investments alongside lead investors, and selective secondaries
STAGES: Series A to pre IPO; primary and secondary transactions; SPV co investments accepted when the lead is institutional
TICKET SIZE: €2–10M initial ticket; reserves of 1x the initial ticket for follow ons; maximum 10% of committed capital in any one company
TARGET OWNERSHIP: 3–15% fully diluted for direct investments; no minimum for co investments and secondaries
GEOGRAPHY: Europe as the core market (at least 60% of capital); up to 40% in North America, the Middle East and other markets where a European angle exists
INDUSTRIES: fintech (payments, lending and embedded credit, banking infrastructure, wealth and insurance technology), B2B software for financial services, and data infrastructure serving regulated industries
TARGET RETURNS: fund level 3.0x gross MOIC; per deal, base case of at least 2.0x gross MOIC and 20% gross IRR, and a bear case that returns at least 0.5x of capital
HOLDING PERIOD: 3–6 years per investment
EXCLUSIONS: companies without a regulatory path in their core market; crypto token issuance; gambling; weapons; tobacco; businesses with unresolved sanctions exposure
GOVERNANCE EXPECTATIONS: information rights on every investment (quarterly management accounts, annual audited accounts, budget); board observer from €5M ticket; board seat from 10% ownership; standard minority protections (pre emption, tag along, reserved matters on new share classes and changes to the business)
ESG: exclusion list screening before term sheet; sanctions and background screening of founders and major shareholders before closing

## Portfolio synergies
Assess fit with the fund's thematic focus (financial infrastructure and embedded finance). Do not name or assume specific portfolio companies; describe synergies only in terms of the strategy above.

## IC process
- Investment committee of five members; approval requires a simple majority, and unanimity for tickets above 7.5% of committed capital.
- Two stages: a screening discussion on the one-pager, then a final decision on the full memo after confirmatory due diligence.
- What the IC usually pushes back on: management plans with no haircut, entry multiples above listed peers without a growth justification, unclear position in the liquidation preference stack, single funding line dependence for credit businesses, and missing net (after fees and carry) returns for SPV deals.
