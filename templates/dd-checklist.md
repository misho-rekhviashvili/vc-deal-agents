# Standard data room checklist

The librarian compares the data room against this list to build the MISSING section of 00-index.md.
Each line: item | workstream | priority (P1 = needed before IC, P2 = needed before signing, P3 = nice to have).

## Financial (FIN)
- Audited financial statements, last 2–3 years | FIN | P1
- Management accounts, current year to date (monthly P&L) | FIN | P1
- Financial model / business plan with assumptions | FIN | P1
- Budget vs actual for the last 12 months | FIN | P2
- Unit economics: CAC, payback, contribution margin, LTV by segment or channel | FIN | P1
- Cohort data: revenue or volume retention by cohort | FIN | P1
- Cash position, burn and runway | FIN | P1
- Debt and funding facilities, covenants and their current headroom | FIN | P1
- Credit performance (lending, BNPL and other credit businesses): default, loss, delinquency, vintage curves | FIN | P1
- Revenue concentration: top 10 customers as % of revenue | FIN | P2
- Tax position and open disputes | FIN | P3

## Legal and corporate (LEG)
- Current fully diluted cap table, including ESOP, warrants, convertibles | LEG | P1
- Articles of association / by laws | LEG | P1
- Existing shareholders' agreement and previous round investment agreements | LEG | P1
- Term sheet for this round | LEG | P1
- Company registry extract (certificate of incorporation / good standing) | LEG | P2
- Material contracts: top customers, key partners, suppliers, bank / funding partners | LEG | P2
- Licences and regulatory authorisations, and correspondence with regulators | LEG | P1
- Litigation, claims and regulatory proceedings | LEG | P2
- IP ownership and assignment from founders and employees | LEG | P2
- Data protection / GDPR compliance | LEG | P3

## Commercial and market (COM)
- Pitch deck / information memorandum | COM | P1
- Product overview and roadmap | COM | P1
- Customer list or anonymised top customers with volumes | COM | P1
- Sales pipeline | COM | P2
- Market study / TAM analysis | COM | P2
- Competitive landscape | COM | P2
- Pricing and take rate by segment | COM | P1
- Customer references or churn analysis | COM | P2

## Team, governance and operations (TEAM)
- Founder and senior management bios | TEAM | P1
- Organisational chart | TEAM | P2
- Hiring plan and headcount budget | TEAM | P2
- ESOP plan and allocation | TEAM | P2
- Board composition and board minutes | TEAM | P2
- Key person dependencies and employment terms of founders | TEAM | P2
- Compliance framework: AML/KYC, risk policies, outsourcing | TEAM | P1
- Background / sanctions checks on founders and major shareholders | TEAM | P1
- IT security, certifications (SOC2, ISO 27001, PCI DSS) | TEAM | P3
