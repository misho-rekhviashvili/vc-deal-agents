# IC memo template

A standard VC investment committee memo structure.
Write every section in this order with exactly these headings. Target length: 2,500 to 3,500 words.
Section guidance is in italics; do not copy the italics into the memo.

# <Company>
Investment Memo
<Month Year>
Strictly confidential: prepared from data room documents as of <date of latest document>

## Deal Team Recommendation
*Three to five sentences: what we are asking IC to approve, at what price, the one line thesis,
the main risk and why we accept it, conditions precedent we require.*

## Investment Rationale
*Three to four paragraphs. Each paragraph = one thesis pillar from 02-key-findings, opened by a
bold one line claim, then the evidence in compact bracketed form. End with the ask.*

## Portfolio Fit / Impact
*Fit with fund/mandate.md on sector, stage, ticket, geography; synergies with named portfolio
companies. If no synergy is supported by the findings, say so in one sentence.*

## Business Overview
*Founding, HQ, founders, what the product does, how it makes money, how it is funded.*

## Product Description
*One paragraph per product line: what it is, scale today, role in the plan.*

## Key Clients
*Customer segments, named top clients if disclosed, concentration, retention.*

## Market Overview
*Market context, then a sizing table. Separate management sizing from our own or third party sizing.*
| Size | Value | Comment |
|---|---|---|

## Competition
*Direct and indirect competitors, why the company wins or could lose, a unit economics
comparison against listed peers where 03-market-check supports it.*

## Financial Analysis
*Historical performance: revenue, gross margin, EBITDA, burn, cash; quality of revenue;
credit or unit economics where relevant. Flag every MGMT CLAIM vs VERIFIED distinction that matters.*
| € million, unless stated | FY-2 A | FY-1 A | FY0 E | FY+1 F |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Business Plan
*Management plan vs our central case: the haircut we apply and why, by driver.*

## Valuation and Return Analysis
*Entry valuation and implied multiples; scenario table copied from 04-returns (do not recompute);
what has to be true for the base case; sensitivity commentary; liquidation preference effect in the downside.*
| Scenario | Exit revenue | EV/Revenue | Gross MOIC | Gross IRR |
|---|---|---|---|---|

## Exit Considerations
*Most likely exit routes, named plausible acquirers with the reason each would buy, IPO conditions, timing.*

## Management Team Overview
*One short paragraph per founder / key executive: role, prior roles, relevant achievement. Then the investor syndicate.*
